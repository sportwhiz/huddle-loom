# Community conduct

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss the work directly, give useful feedback, and leave room for people with different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For a sensitive conduct concern, open an issue asking a maintainer to arrange a private conversation. Leave out names and personal details until a private channel has been agreed.
